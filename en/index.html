Privacy

What leaves the phone, and what doesn't.

Your subscriptions — names, amounts, dates, notes — never leave the phone. There are no accounts, no server and no cloud copy.

The only thing sent out is anonymous usage statistics plus crash reports, so we know what to fix. They carry no identity of yours and none of your subscription content, and they can be turned off in Settings.
