Your subscriptions, on your phone and nowhere else.

Zubscriptions keeps track of everything that renews on you: how much it is, when it lands and what share is yours. No account to create, no bank to connect, nothing uploaded to any server.

In closed testing · coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

What it does

  • It warns you before, not after

    A reminder 7, 3 or 1 day before the charge — or whenever you say — set per subscription. The alert arrives while there's still time to cancel.

  • The real total

    Yearly plans spread out, weekly ones added up: everything converted to the same period so you can see what it costs per day, week, month or year.

  • Where it all goes

    Spending by category, month-over-month trend, a calendar of upcoming charges and a ranking of what costs you most.

  • Personal and business, kept apart

    Mark each subscription as personal or work, and look at the two sets of numbers separately whenever you need to.

  • What you share, split

    The family Netflix divided four ways: note who's on it and how much you put in, and every total counts only your share.

  • Your data leaves with you

    Export everything to a file whenever you like and import it back on another phone. No lock-in, no permission needed.

What leaves the phone, and what doesn't

Your subscriptions — names, amounts, dates, notes — never leave the phone. There are no accounts, no server and no cloud copy: everything is stored in a local database inside the app.

The only thing sent out is anonymous usage statistics — which screens are opened, which buttons are tapped — plus crash reports, so we know what to fix. They carry no identity of yours and none of your subscription content, and they can be turned off in Settings.

It's spelled out, with no fine print, in the privacy policy.

Free, with one optional purchase

The first 7 subscriptions are free, reminders and totals included. If you need more, Zubscriptions Pro is a one-time purchase — or a quarterly one — that lifts the cap. No ads, no data selling, no hidden subscription: you'll see the exact price inside the app before paying anything.
