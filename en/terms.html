Pro is an optional in-app purchase that lifts the cap and enables additional features. It is offered in two forms:

The exact price, currency and duration are shown in the app before you confirm, and are set by the store for your country. The charge is made by Apple or Google, not by us, using the payment method on your store account.

If the purchase stops being active, your recorded subscriptions are not deleted: the free-version limit applies again for adding new ones, and you can still view and export everything you already had.
