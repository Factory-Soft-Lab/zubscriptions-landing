Privacy policy

Effective 11 August 2026 · Applies to the Zubscriptions mobile app for iOS and Android.

Zubscriptions has no user accounts and no server of our own. What you record — what you pay, how much and when — stays inside the app, on your phone. This document explains the one exception and how to switch it off.

1. Who is responsible for this data

The data controller is Factory Soft Lab S.A.S., a company domiciled in Colombia and the developer of Zubscriptions. Anything related to this policy is handled at support@fabtorysoftlab.com.

2. What we don't collect

To get this out of the way first, Zubscriptions does not:

  • ask for sign-up, email, phone number or password — there are no user accounts;
  • connect to your bank or to the services you pay for, and never reads transactions;
  • store card numbers: the "payment method" is just a label you type;
  • access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or calendar;
  • show ads or use advertising identifiers (no IDFA, no iOS tracking prompt);
  • sell, rent or hand your data to anyone.

3. What stays on your phone

Everything you record in the app is stored in a local database, in the private space the operating system reserves for Zubscriptions: service names, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, dates, categories, notes, benefits, participants in a shared subscription and your preferences.

None of it is transmitted. There is no cloud copy, no sync between devices, and no way for us to see it. If the phone is lost or you uninstall the app, that data goes with it — which is why the export exists (section 9).

4. What is sent

The only information that leaves the device is usage statistics and crash reports, processed with Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google). They tell us what gets used and what breaks.

What is sentExample
Screens opened screen_view with a fixed name from a closed list: home, dashboard, settings…
Buttons tapped button_tap with the screen and the button name, also from a closed list
Actions in the app, without their content that a subscription was created, paused or archived; that an export or import happened; that the purchase screen was viewed
How many subscriptions you have, in bands 4_7, 8_15, 31_PLUS — never the exact figure
Technical crash data device model, OS version, app version and the error trace

What these events never carry: a service name, an amount, a date, a note, a participant, or any text you typed. The app also assigns no user identifier and no profile attributes. An event says "a subscription was created", never "Netflix was created for 44,900 on the 3rd".

Firebase assigns an installation identifier to the device and records the approximate country inferred from the IP address, as part of how the tool normally works. That identifier resets if you uninstall the app or turn statistics off.

5. How to switch it off

Under Settings → Privacy → Usage statistics there is a single switch that turns off both the statistics and the crash reports. It starts on; turning it off stops any sending immediately and it does not resume until you turn it back on. The preference is stored on the phone and read before Firebase starts, so off means off on the next launch too.

You can use the whole app with the switch off. No feature is lost.

6. Who it's shared with

Only with Google LLC, as the provider of Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and only the data in section 4. Google processes it under its own terms; see firebase.google.com/support/privacy and the Google privacy policy.

This means an international transfer: the information is processed on Google servers outside Colombia, including in the United States. Using the app with statistics enabled authorises that transfer; if you would rather avoid it, switch them off.

We use no other third-party tooling: no ad networks, no maps, no additional analytics SDKs.

7. How long it's kept

  • What's on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app. It does not expire.
  • Usage events: kept in Firebase for at most 14 months, per the retention period configured on the project.
  • Crash reports: around 90 days, which is when Crashlytics discards them.

8. In-app purchases

If you buy Zubscriptions Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple or Google, depending on the store. We neither see nor receive your name, email, address or payment details: the app only asks the operating system whether the purchase is active. Processing of that data is the responsibility of the store you paid through.

9. Export and import

The app can produce a file with all your subscriptions so you can back them up or move them to another phone. You create that file and it goes wherever you send it — email, messaging, whichever cloud you use — and from that point it falls under the terms of the service you chose. We receive no copy and play no part in that transfer.

10. Notifications

Charge reminders are scheduled and fired on the phone itself. No notification is sent from a server, so there is no external record of your billing dates. If you don't grant notification permission, the rest of the app works just the same.

11. Children

Zubscriptions is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a minor in your care has used the app and want us to look into something, write to us.

12. Your rights

Under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 and its implementing rules, you have the right to know, update and correct your data, to request proof of your authorisation, to be informed about its use, to file complaints with the Superintendency of Industry and Commerce, to revoke your authorisation and to request deletion.

In practice you exercise almost all of those yourself, without asking us for anything: we don't hold data that identifies you, and what you record lives on your phone, where you can view, correct, export or delete it at any time. For the part that does depend on us — the usage events — write to support@fabtorysoftlab.com and we'll respond within the statutory deadlines.

13. How to delete everything

Uninstalling the app removes all of your information, leaving nothing of ours on any server: there is no account to close because there never was one. Inside the app you can also archive or delete subscriptions one by one. To stop us receiving usage events as well, turn off the switch in section 5 before uninstalling.

14. Changes to this policy

If something relevant changes we'll update this page and the effective date in the header. When the change affects what is collected, we'll also say so inside the app before it takes effect.
