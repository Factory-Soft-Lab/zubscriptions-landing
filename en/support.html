Frequently asked questions

Does Zubscriptions cancel my subscriptions?

No. The app keeps the record and warns you before the charge, but cancelling has to be done with each provider. That's also why you can set the reminder 7, 3 or 1 day ahead: so there's time.

Is my data in the cloud?

No. Everything is stored on your phone, in a local database inside the app. There are no accounts and no server, so there's no automatic backup either. It's explained in the privacy policy.

I'm switching phones — how do I take everything with me?

Under Settings → Export the app produces a file with all your subscriptions. Save it or send it to yourself, then use Settings → Import on the new phone. It's worth doing occasionally even if you're not switching.

Reminders aren't arriving.

It's almost always notification permission or battery saving. Check that Zubscriptions has permission in the system settings, that it isn't restricted in the background, and that low-power mode is off. If they still don't arrive, write to us with your phone model.

How do I turn usage statistics off?

Under Settings → Privacy → Usage statistics. Turning it off stops events and crash reports immediately, and you lose no features.

How do I delete all my information?

By uninstalling the app. Since there's no account and no server, that's all it takes. Export first if you want to keep a copy.

I bought Pro and it isn't showing.

Use Restore purchases on the Pro screen, with the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with. If it still doesn't show, write to us with the store and the purchase date.

How do I cancel the quarterly subscription?

From your store account settings: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.
